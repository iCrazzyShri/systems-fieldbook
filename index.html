02 / SYSTEMATIC RESEARCHEXPERIMENTAL

QuantSmart

An exploration of quantitative strategies and systematic research. The interesting part is testing an idea carefully enough to discover where it stops holding up.

The research lens: assumptions, data quality, validation and risk. Strategies remain experimental; a backtest is a question to investigate, not evidence of future profitability.

03 / AI & PRODUCT BUILDINGEXPLORATION

Personal AI & agents

Exploring how AI agents can become useful collaborators in real work. The goal is practical help, with enough visibility to understand the reasoning behind the next step.

The design questions: What context does an agent need? Which tools should it use? Where does human judgment belong? How do we make its actions inspectable and recoverable?
